# Demo video: script and shot list

Target length **2 minutes 30 seconds**. Shorter is better than complete.

Format: a presenter on camera for the opening and close, screen recording for everything
in between, voiceover over the screen. If the presenter is an AI avatar, record the
screen captures first, because the voiceover has to match what is actually on screen.

Rule for the whole video: **never show the product before the problem lands.** The
capstone review failed six of nine pitches for opening with the build.

---

## Shot 1 · Presenter, no screen · 0:00 to 0:22

> A family in Hyderabad decides to buy a home.
>
> Over the next four months they will be worked by two or three channel partner firms,
> a sales executive at every project they enquire about, a site executive who shows them
> the flat, and someone from the builder's office.
>
> Every one of those people keeps their own list.

Pause. Then:

> So the family answers the same five questions four times. Two partners discover they
> registered the same phone number and argue about the commission on WhatsApp. And the
> builder, who owns the inventory and paid for the advertising, has to ask somebody for a
> spreadsheet to find out how his own project sold this week.

---

## Shot 2 · Presenter · 0:22 to 0:38

> The record of that family exists in everybody's sheet and nobody's system.
>
> That is the whole problem. Not effort, not software. Most of these people already own a
> CRM. The family has no identity, so there is nothing for the work to accumulate against.
>
> So we gave the family one.

---

## Shot 3 · Screen · 0:38 to 1:05 · the record

Screen record: open one family record in the Lead Engine. Scroll it slowly.

> One permanent id, issued the first time a family touches us. Every later touch writes
> against it.
>
> The ad they clicked and the creative it was. Every call and what came of it. The site
> visit. The budget they stated and the budget they actually have. The cheque. The
> booking. And when they come back eighteen months later for the second property, it is
> all still here.

Show the `client_id` field clearly. That is the hero of the video.

---

## Shot 4 · Screen · 1:05 to 1:35 · attribution

Screen record: the partner desk. Show a lead marked `duplicate`, then a `contested` one
with its audit log entry.

> Two hundred and thirty four partner firms register buyers here. They compete with each
> other, and they do not trust each other.
>
> First registration owns the buyer for ninety days. A second firm registering the same
> number is marked duplicate automatically. Where both have a real claim it goes to
> arbitration, and the decision is written to a log that nobody can edit, including me.
>
> Twenty four duplicates caught. Three disputes decided. Before this, the same arguments
> happened on WhatsApp and nobody wrote down the outcome.

---

## Shot 5 · Screen · 1:35 to 1:58 · the public layer

Screen record: hyderabad.properties. Search a project, open a report, show sources and
dates. Then show the review desk with something pending approval.

> This part is public and needs no login. It reads the records nobody reads. RERA
> registrations and orders, building permits, lake boundaries, government guideline
> values.
>
> Claude reads the new documents. A person approves every verdict before it publishes,
> because being wrong in public about somebody's project is a lawsuit, not a bad metric.
>
> And a buyer who checks a project here becomes a family record in the same engine. That
> closes the loop.

---

## Shot 6 · Screen or title cards · 1:58 to 2:15 · traction

Hold each number for about two seconds. Do not narrate all of them.

> Nine thousand one hundred families on one record. Six thousand four hundred of them
> lifted out of spreadsheets in a single backfill on the sixth of October. Thirty to forty
> thousand contacts logged against them. Twenty seven people using it every working day.
> Seventy four bookings closed on it.

---

## Shot 7 · Presenter · 2:15 to 2:30 · the honest close

> What I have not measured yet is the minutes this saves per family. I know the method,
> it takes an afternoon, and it is the next thing I do.
>
> What I can show is this. Six thousand four hundred families who used to be rows in a
> spreadsheet now have one record each, two hundred and thirty four firms that compete
> with each other work against it, and the argument that used to happen on WhatsApp is
> now a decision with a log.

End card: **One record for one family. So nobody asks the same question twice.**

---

## Production notes

**Record the screen first.** Six captures, in order: a family record scrolled slowly,
a duplicate lead, a contested lead with its audit entry, hyderabad.properties search and
report, the review desk, the bookings view.

**Blur or replace every real name and phone number.** These are real families. Use a
staging record, or blur in post. This is not optional.

**Backup.** The capstone pitch template asks for a recorded demo in case the network
fails on the day. This video is that backup. Keep it under three minutes.

**Voice.** Plain and flat. No music swell under the numbers, no adjectives. The numbers
carry it or they do not.
