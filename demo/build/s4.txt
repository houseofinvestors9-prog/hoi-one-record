Two hundred and thirty four partner firms register buyers here. They compete with each other and they do not trust each other.
First registration owns the buyer for ninety days. A second firm registering the same number gets marked duplicate automatically. Where both have a real claim it goes to arbitration, and the decision is written to a log that nobody can edit, including me.
Twenty four duplicates caught. Three disputes decided. Before this, the same fights happened on WhatsApp and nobody wrote down the outcome.
