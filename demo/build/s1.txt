A family in Hyderabad decides to buy a flat.
Over the next four months they will be worked by two or three partner firms, a sales executive at every project they enquire about, a site executive who shows them the flat, and someone from the builder's office.
Every one of them keeps a list of their own.
So by the third call the family has stopped answering properly. Two firms find out they registered the same number and fight about the commission on WhatsApp. And the builder, who is paying for the ads and owns the flats, has to ask somebody for a sheet to find out how his own project sold this week.
